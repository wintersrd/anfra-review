How to read it

HR decoupling compares aerobic efficiency (pace per heart rate) in the first half of a long run against the second half. Values near 0% mean you held pace at a steady heart rate; larger positive values mean heart rate drifted up relative to pace — a sign of heat, hydration or aerobic fatigue. The recent-average tile above covers the last 90 days; the chart shows every qualifying long run.

Decoupling is computed only for runs of 75 minutes or more.
