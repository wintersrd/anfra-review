The date range drives every chart on its own grain. The ride-type picker and clicking a slice in the ride-mix donut focus the activity-based charts and tiles — except fitness & form (all sports, full history capped at the range end), weekly cycling load, weekly hours by type, weekly distance, the calendar and both decoupling views, which ignore the type focus by design. The tiles use fixed wall-clock windows (this week; last 90 days), so the date range does not move them. The default view is the last 90 days of data; re-picking a preset recalculates from today.

How to read the power charts

FTP comes from the ride itself (intervals.icu per-activity FTP); the tile shows the latest one. %FTP = weighted watts ÷ FTP, binned left-inclusive: Z1 <55%, Z2 55–75%, Z3 75–90%, Z4 90–105%, Z5 105–120%, Z6 ≥120%.

IF (intensity factor) = weighted watts ÷ FTP per ride; the monthly line weights each ride by its moving time. W/kg divides the week's weighted watts by body weight: that week's average, else the most recent earlier week. Weeks without a known weight show a gap in W/kg only.
